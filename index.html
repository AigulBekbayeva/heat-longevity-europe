#30DayMapChallenge · Heat & Longevity in Europe

Long lives, hot summers

Three maps of Europe's regions. The first shows where people live the longest and the second where the last decade was hottest. The third shows where the two collide: hot regions with many older people, the group most vulnerable to extreme heat.

          Map 03

          Heat Inequality: Who Suffers Most?

          Older people are the most vulnerable to heat. Each region is placed in a third by heat (the measure picked in Map 02) and a third by the share of people aged 65+. The darkest regions are in the hottest third and the oldest third at once.

          Hot and old

          Hottest third × oldest third, ranked by the combined percentile.

            Data: heat from Map 02; share of population aged 65+ from Eurostat (demo_r_pjanind2).

            NUTS 2 regions (NUTS 2024) of the EU, EFTA and candidate countries. The UK isn't in the 2024 NUTS classification and is shown in grey along with other countries outside it. The Azores, Madeira, the Canary Islands, Svalbard and the French overseas regions aren't shown. Borders: Eurostat GISCO via Nuts2json, Lambert azimuthal equal-area projection. Cities: Natural Earth.
